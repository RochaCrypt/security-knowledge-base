> ISACA · enterprise IT risk management and control.

## 🔎 What it is

An ISACA certification for professionals who identify and manage IT risk and design the controls that address it. Strongly valued in risk, governance and compliance roles.

## 🎯 What it validates

- IT risk governance
- Risk assessment
- Risk response and reporting
- Information technology and security controls

## 🧠 Key concepts & definitions

| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Inherent risk** | Risk before any controls are applied |
| **Residual risk** | Risk remaining after controls |
| **Risk appetite** | How much risk the organisation accepts |
| **Control** | A measure that reduces risk |
| **KRI** | Key Risk Indicator — a metric signalling rising risk |
