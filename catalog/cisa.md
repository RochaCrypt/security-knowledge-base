# 🔍 CISA — Certified Information Systems Auditor

[🏠 Profile](https://github.com/RochaCrypt) · [📚 Catalog](../README.md) · <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/📊_Grc-0d1117?style=flat-square&labelColor=0d1117" alt="grc"/>

> ISACA · the standard for IT audit, control and assurance.

## 🔎 What it is

ISACA's audit-focused certification. Proves you can assess IT systems, controls and governance and report on their effectiveness. The go-to cert for IT auditors and GRC roles.

## 🎯 What it validates

- The information systems audit process
- Governance and management of IT
- Acquisition, development and implementation
- Operations, resilience and protection of assets

## 🧠 Key concepts & definitions

| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Control** | A safeguard that reduces risk (preventive, detective, corrective) |
| **Audit evidence** | Information gathered to support audit conclusions |
| **Materiality** | Whether a finding is significant enough to matter |
| **Segregation of duties** | Splitting tasks so no one person can commit and hide fraud |
| **Compensating control** | An alternative control when the primary one isn't feasible |
