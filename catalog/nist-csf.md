# 🏗️ NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0

[🏠 Profile](https://github.com/RochaCrypt) · [📚 Catalog](../README.md) · <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/🧭_Framework-0d1117?style=flat-square&labelColor=0d1117" alt="framework"/>

> A risk-based framework for managing cybersecurity at any scale.

## 🔎 What it is

A voluntary, widely adopted framework from NIST that organises cybersecurity into core functions. Version 2.0 (2024) added Govern, making six functions in total, and applies to organisations of any size.

## 🎯 What it validates

- Structures security programmes around 6 functions
- Links business governance to technical controls
- Supports risk assessment and maturity tiers
- Maps to other standards like ISO 27001

## 🧠 Key concepts & definitions

| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Govern** | New in 2.0: strategy, roles, policy and oversight |
| **Identify** | Understand assets, risks and business context |
| **Protect** | Safeguards to limit or contain impact |
| **Detect** | Find events and anomalies |
| **Respond & Recover** | Act on incidents and restore normal operations |
